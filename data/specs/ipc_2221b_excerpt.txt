IPC-2221B Section 6.1
Minimum electrical clearance between external conductors shall be maintained according to operating voltage.

For low-voltage traces around 5V logic domains:
- keep spacing at or above 0.10 mm
- avoid residual copper bridges between SDA/SCL lines and neighboring GND references
- verify etching and wash process to prevent conductive residue

Violation impact:
- bus arbitration loss
- stuck-low data line
- intermittent communication faults under load
